1 Connect your keyboard

Looking for your keyboard...

Please check these things:

  • Plug the keyboard into the computer with the USB-C cable.
  • Slide the switch on the back of the keyboard to USB. Bluetooth and the 2.4G dongle can't control the screen.
  • Your computer is not letting this program talk to the keyboard yet. This is a permission setting on your computer. Ask whoever set up your computer, or see the help page of this tool.

This also sets the clock shown on the keyboard screen to your computer's time.
